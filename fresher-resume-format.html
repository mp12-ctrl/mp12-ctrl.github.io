Best Fresher Resume Format

Freshers often face difficulty while creating a resume because of limited experience. A simple and clean resume format helps freshers get shortlisted easily.

1. Personal Information

  • Full Name
  • Mobile Number
  • Email Address
  • City, State

2. Career Objective

Write a short and clear objective explaining your career goals and interest in the job. Keep it simple and professional.

3. Educational Qualification

Mention your highest qualification first. Include:

  • Course / Degree
  • Board or University
  • Passing Year
  • Percentage or CGPA

4. Skills Section

Add technical skills, computer skills, or soft skills related to the job profile. Avoid adding unnecessary skills.

5. Projects / Internship (Optional)

If you have done any academic project or internship, mention it briefly with key responsibilities.

6. Personal Details

  • Date of Birth
  • Gender
  • Nationality
  • Languages Known

7. Declaration

I hereby declare that the information given above is true and correct to the best of my knowledge.
