Best Resume Tips for Freshers & Job Seekers

A resume is the first step toward getting a job. A professional and well-structured resume helps you stand out.

1. Keep Your Resume Simple

Use a clean layout and easy-to-read font. Avoid unnecessary colors and long paragraphs.

2. Add Correct Personal Details

Always mention a valid phone number and professional email address.

3. Write a Clear Career Objective

Explain your job goal and key skills in short and simple words.

4. Mention Relevant Skills

Add only those skills that are related to the job profile.

5. Education Details Should Be Clear

Mention qualification, board or university, year, and percentage.

6. Avoid False Information

Never add fake experience or skills in your resume.
